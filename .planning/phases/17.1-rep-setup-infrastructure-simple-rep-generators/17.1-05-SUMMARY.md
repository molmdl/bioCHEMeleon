- **Found during:** Task 2 (first smoke run)
- **Issue:** 3/5 hiders on 1znf exhausted the 25 full-sep tries and triggered 17.1-03's pinned relaxation contract (halve all seps × 2 rounds → accept final draw): observed nearest-real-atom distances 0.67/0.92/0.39 Å — below the plan's asserted [1.19, 1.61] band. ### Discovery (for 17.1-06 / phase-17 verification / future tuning — no action taken here)

**Relaxation on crowded anchors is COMMON, not exceptional.** On 1znf (424-atom NMR structure with hydrogens), 1-3 of 5 hiders per round exhaust the 25 full-sep tries and accept a relaxed candidate that can sit INSIDE a non-anchor real atom's VDW radius (observed 0.39-0.92 Å vs C radius 1.70). Consequences: (a) the hider still auto-bonds (numbonds ≥ 1 proven both runs — the anchor bond is unaffected); (b) under VDW/CPK a relaxed hider renders nearly buried in its neighbor — a cosmetic blend-quality degradation, arguably still "blends in"; (c) tuning candidates for a later plan (NOT 17.1-05's scope — they would alter 17.1-03's TDD-pinned constants): larger MAX_TRIES, excluding H from the neighborhood fetch, or excluding bonded neighbors of the anchor from the MIN_SEP_REAL set. 17.1-06's dispatch and the GUI verify sessions should expect relaxed placements as normal.
