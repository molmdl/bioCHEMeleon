## Decisions Made
- **Line-based .dat parser with Directional_Light filtering** (see key-decisions): the plan's suggested whole-file `Color` regex would have caught the two light lines' `Color 1 1 1`; the plan-prescribed first-render inspection caught it before pinning.

- **Radius mimicry double-assert**: pinned VDW table (eps 1e-6) AND real same-element radius read pre-start -- immune to table drift, still plan-exact.
- **Cleanup robustness**: flat guarded blocks so `game::cleanup` always runs once a round exists (no leaked game molecule on a FAIL), unlike the dispatch smoke's deep nesting.
- **Scene-diff isolation**: hidden/found/probe reps emptied; only scene reps toggled (`all` <-> `not resname GAM`) -- A/B attributable to the scene rep alone; `mark_found_visual`'s unchanged-string re-assert then restores the pair selections for free.
