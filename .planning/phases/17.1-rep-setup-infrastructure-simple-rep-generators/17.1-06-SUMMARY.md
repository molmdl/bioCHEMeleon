- **Empty-slice guard in the tier split:** a tier producing 0 records gets no tier_of entry (stamping `index ` with an empty list would error); its rep pair is still added (empty selection harmless, research RQ3.3). Defensive -- resolve_per_rep cannot currently emit it.
