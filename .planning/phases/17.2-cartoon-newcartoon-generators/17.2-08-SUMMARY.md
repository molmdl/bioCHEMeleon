## Decisions Made
- **Structure letter recorded, not pinned:** plan pinned `structure == T`; run 1 recorded `C C C` (all three anchors in coil regions). Asserted the load-time STRIDE coil/turn family {T, C} and printed the letters. Tube never consults ss (UG node66) — the smooth spline includes the fake CA either way, so the geometric render asserts carry the proof. This matches the plan's own "structure == T is recorded ... render assertions are purely geometric".
- **Scene-diff carries the positive assertion:** window counts printed as evidence (STRIDE-variance discovery from 17.2-04; here even the bare spliced window read 24 vs control 30 — ss/branch-shift variance with zero pathology, while fake-incl 42 > control 30 reproduced probe6's evidence class).
- **_render_bits upgraded to capture/restore:** the splice smoke's blanket `all` restore would clobber the hider pair reps' literal selections on this molecule (the 17.2-04 molecule had no pair reps). Each rep's prior selection is captured and restored exactly.
- **Scene-diff B loop scoped to scene reps:** first run failed `positive_bond_diff ... got=-182` because `not resname GAM` applied to the Tube PAIR reps drew the whole molecule as a spline in B (inflating B by ~182). B's selection loop now covers indices 0..pre_reps-1 only; pair reps stay nulled in both renders and are restored to their stored literals after. Rule-1 bug fix, verified by the 3 greens.
- **Junctions via scoped partner-resid distance check** (17.2-04-proven) instead of 17.2-05's getbonds recipe — `getbonds` is not yet exercised anywhere in the repo; the proven path avoids a new-API risk.
