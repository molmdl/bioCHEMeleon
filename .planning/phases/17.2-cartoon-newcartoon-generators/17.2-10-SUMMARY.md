## Decisions Made
- **Draw-adaptive assertions over pinned counts (rounds A and B).** Run 9 proved round A's pinned 439/425/430/435 flakes when `splice::select_anchors` samples two CAs closer than 5.0 A (it samples exactly n candidates then greedy-accepts; "May return fewer than n" is its documented contract) -- ~1 in 9 runs on 28-residue 1znf. The smoke now asserts the deterministic OBSERVED layout (registry == sentinels, block geometry, bounds) and keeps the idealized pins as strict special cases. P9 `hider_count == 3` and the verbatim `{Cartoon 2 Trace 1}` stash remain pinned unconditionally (they are request-side, generation-independent).
- **Chain classifier instead of name classifier.** Bonded hiders copy their anchor's atom name (run 5: an all-simple {Lines 1 CPK 4} draw produced sentinels named `CA NE2 CB N O`), so "name == CA" false-positives. Simple records are hard-coded chain G (`HID_CHAIN`), residue records the anchor's chain (G/blank rejected as anchors) -- chain is the reliable public discriminator.
- **Strict x5 formula gated on `cb_extra == res` (not 0).** `cb_extra = numatoms - orig - simple - 4*res` counts CB-carrying blocks; the idealized formula needs EVERY block to carry its CB.
