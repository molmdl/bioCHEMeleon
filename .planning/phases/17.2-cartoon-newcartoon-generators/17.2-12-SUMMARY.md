## Decisions Made
- **Fresh 1znf as the round-3 target.** The residue splice requires protein CA anchors; 1k8p (rounds 1-2's demo) is DNA-only and would degrade all four residue tiers to 0 records (17.2-09 supply-0 policy). 1znf is the bundled default protein demo the headless dispatch smoke already proved residue rounds on.
- **Stash refresh inside pv_round3.** ::pv_gs is captured at the FIRST pv_state dump and never refreshed; after the 16-13 guard restarts deleted rounds 1-2 game molids, the final pv_cleanup would fail (backup::restore mol delete on a dead molid) and the round-3 molecule would stay mutated in the scene. Unsetting before on_start lets the round-3 dump re-stash the live state (Rule 2 auto-fix, see Deviations).
- **9005 negative control + logged-not-asserted SS letters.** The resid-block dump doubles as a miss-path check; structure letters stay data ({T, C} family), consistent with the smoke precedent.
- **Instructions lead with the first-click quirk** (step 0: press p once BEFORE any click -- clicks before one p press land in labelatom mode, locked contract in vmd/AGENTS.md). The pv_instructions block grew to 12 short lines (plan said <= 8) to fit the quirk lead + the full 3-round route; every line stays short and everything else remains auto-issued/auto-logged (the standing 16-16 directive).
