## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 17.2 is the hardest generator tier, but the research **de-risks it to a single well-characterized mechanism**: write ONE fake residue (`GAM`, full backbone N/CA/C/O/CB) into the combined PDB, copied from a real anchor residue's coordinates and **rigid-translated by 1.0 Å perpendicular to the local peptide bond**, with the **anchor's chain id** and a **disjoint resid block (9001+k)**. Probed end-to-end on 1xdn (single-model protein) and 1znf model-1 (the default demo): the fake residue is classified **`protein`** (VMD classifies by backbone atoms + connectivity, not resname — GAM is not in `restypes.dat` yet classifies as protein), is **distance-bonded into the chain at both junctions**, is **traced by Cartoon, NewCartoon, Trace AND Tube alike** (one splice serves all four reps), and renders as a **connected smooth-tube bump**.

The headline resolution of the phase's central caveat: **there is no `ss='L'` in VMD** — that is PyMOL vocabulary. VMD's per-atom secondary structure (readable via the `atomselect` keyword `structure`) is `T/C/H/G/E/B` (turn/coil/helix/3-10/extended/bridge), and a spliced GAM residue gets **`T` (turn)** from the load-time STRIDE run — which renders as the smooth coil/turn tube, i.e. **exactly the loop-tube look v1 fought for and the user accepted on 2026-08-16**. `mol ssrecalc` exists but is **unnecessary** (load-time STRIDE already assigns ss) and **destructive** (it wipes manual ss writes). The HIDER-05 premise "splice with `mol ssrecalc`" therefore dissolves: splice yes, ssrecalc no.

Two findings upgrade the caveat from "accepted limitation" to "characterized mechanism": (1) VMD's load-time **bond law `d < 0.6 × (r_i + r_j)`** [CITED, 17.1-RESEARCH-simple-generators.md] exactly predicts my measured displacement envelope — the peptide junction (1.33 Å real, 1.95 Å C–N cutoff) survives displacement `d` perpendicular to it while `sqrt(1.33² + d²) < 1.95`, i.e. **d < 1.43 Å**; probes measured 1.0 Å safe and 1.44 Å broken in perfect agreement. (2) **A manual ss override EXISTS** — `atomselect set structure H` is writable and **the Cartoon renderer honors it** (E→H flipped the window render from sheet-ribbon triangles to helix cylinders, probe8) — but the UG's **≥3-residue helix rule** (a 1–2 residue "helix" draws as coil) means a single-residue hider renders as a tube *no matter what ss is written*; force-SS is only meaningful for a ≥3-residue fake segment (a real future polish — the v1 11-08 deferred "SS copy" is achievable in v2).

**Primary recommendation:** Build ONE shared GAM-backbone-residue splice generator serving Cartoon/NewCartoon/Trace/Tube; accept the loop/turn-tube rendering (Option A) for 17.2; **do NOT call `mol ssrecalc`** in the generation flow; record the ss caveat with the probe8 upgrade (manual override possible, single-residue exempt by the ≥3-residue rule). Sentinel stays `resname GAM` + `beta=-999 on CA only` + `segid GAME` — the canonical selector `resname GAM and beta < 0` is UNCHANGED and returns exactly one index per hider.

## 2. Q1 — Cartoon/NewCartoon/Trace/Tube rendering requirements & GAM classification

### 2.1 What the four reps draw [CITED — UG §6.1.10/6.1.11/6.1.14/6.1.15, extracted from vmd-ref/ug.pdf]

| Rep | UG description | Geometry driver | SS-dependent? |
|-----|----------------|-----------------|---------------|
| **Trace** | "connects the alpha-carbon atoms of successive residues by cylindrical segments" | CA–CA, per fragment | **No** |
| **Tube** | "a smooth curve through the selected Cα positions … a spline curve that passes through all the Cα atoms in a protein fragment" | CA spline, per fragment | **No** |
| **Cartoon** | "helices are drawn as cylinders, beta sheets as solid ribbons, and all other structures (coils and turns) as a tube. If the secondary structure has not yet been determined, it will be calculated automatically by the program STRIDE" | CA + STRIDE ss | **Yes (shape only)** |
| **NewCartoon** | "smooth cartoon diagram (smooth ribbons) based on secondary structure" — helices as curved ribbons; "the B-spline does not always pass through the Cα positions" | CA + STRIDE ss | **Yes (shape only)** |

Key UG rules for the design:
- **Helix ≥3-residue rule:** "because this method computes a best fit, a helix must have at least 3 residues before it is drawn (those helicies with one or two residues are drawn as a coil)" [CITED UG §6.1.14; re-PROBED in probe8: writing `H` on ONE residue changed nothing in the render, writing `H` on 6 residues flipped the geometry].
- **Trace precedent for faking:** "people working on polymers can fake their structure by naming everything 'CA' in the pdb file and then using trace" [CITED UG §6.1.10] — the UG itself blesses fake-residue tracing.
- Picking a cartoon helix cylinder targets the Cα location [CITED UG §6.1.14 + 17.1-RESEARCH-game-reps-viability.md node69 row].

### 2.2 How VMD classifies a residue as protein/cartoon-traceable

- The residue-name table `vmd-ref/scripts/restypes.dat` lists the 20 standard amino acids (plus variants) — **GAM is NOT in it** [CITED]. Yet:
- **`atomselect protein` DOES match a spliced GAM backbone residue** [VERIFIED probe2/probe6: `hider_in_protein=5` (all 5 atoms) on 1xdn variants A–H and on 1znf-model-1 splice; `protein_total` rose exactly by the 5 fake atoms]. Sibling 17.1 independently verified the same on its synthetic 4-residue chain [CITED 17.1-RESEARCH-game-reps-viability.md: "residue classification is name/connectivity-based, not resname-based"].
- The load-time analysis classifies by **backbone atom names + distance bonding**, then builds **homogeneous fragments** ("Protein and nucleic fragments are homogeneous; either all proteins, or all nucleic acids" [CITED UG §4.2]). The cartoon/trace/tube reps draw the CA trace **per fragment** — so the fake residue must be **bonded into the chain** to join the protein fragment's trace. Bonding is the load-time distance search (bond law below).
- Load-time STRIDE runs headless: the `structure` keyword was fully populated (`T/C/H/G/E/B` across 1xdn, real helices reading `H`) immediately after `mol new`, before any Cartoon rep was created [VERIFIED probe1/probe2]. This also resolves 17.1's LOW-confidence "headless STRIDE reliability" open question **in the affirmative** (their probes may have hit a different failure mode; on 1xdn/1znf-model-1 STRIDE demonstrably ran and assigned the full alphabet).

### 2.3 Does a non-standard GAM residue with standard backbone names + continuous chain get traced? **YES — probed across all four reps.**

[VERIFIED probe5b/probe6 — 1xdn chain A, GAM (N/CA/C/O/CB) copied from residue 100, rigid +1.0 Å perpendicular to the peptide bond, appended into chain A:]
- **GAME-only Cartoon render:** 6 `FCylinder` primitives (vs 0 for an untraced selection, axes off) — the residue's cartoon tube bits draw.
- **GAME-only NewCartoon:** 14 `TriStrip` mesh primitives (vs 0) — its ribbon mesh draws.

- **Window-include render (Cartoon `{resid 99 to 103}` on the spliced molecule):** FCyl 27 / NewCartoon TriStrip 70 / Trace 8 / Tube 30 — exactly the counts of a 5-path-unit window (the 5-real-residue control), i.e. **the fake residue is IN the traced path**, and at probe6 (disjoint resid 9001) the trace builder even **rerouted the path through the fake residue** (the direct CA→CA segment count dropped while the bump appeared).
- Sibling 17.1 corroborates: Trace drew the CA4→CA5(GAM) segment; Cartoon drew 18 spline cylinders and NewCartoon 42 mesh triangles including the GAM CA [CITED 17.1-RESEARCH-game-reps-viability.md §viability matrix].

### 2.4 Classification/trace requirements distilled (the splice contract)

1. Full backbone atoms with standard names **N, CA, C, O** (+CB for sidechain feel) [VERIFIED].
2. **Chain id must match the host chain** [VERIFIED in-consequence: all probes with chain A bonded+traced; a disjoint chain would form a separate fragment → v1's disconnected-look failure mode. Not explicitly probed with a mismatched chain — **[ASSUMED: verify once in the smoke by deliberately writing chain 'G' and asserting 0 render bits]**].
3. Position within ~1.0 Å of a real residue's backbone so the distance search bonds both junctions (§Q3).
4. Resid **numbering is irrelevant to rendering** [VERIFIED probe2 variant G (resid 999, no renumbering) and probe6 (resid 9001, no renumbering): renders identical to the renumbered variants]. Use a disjoint resid block for registry hygiene.
5. TER cards: never write one inside the chain; appending the fake residue after the last real atom / before the existing TER works [VERIFIED probe2 variant F (append at C-term 316→317, rendered) and probe6 END-placement test].

## 3. Q2 — STRIDE, the ss field, and what "ss='L'" means in VMD

### 3.1 When does VMD run STRIDE?

- **At load** (headless-verified: `structure` populated immediately after `mol new`, before any rep exists) [VERIFIED probe1/probe2] and again **when a Cartoon-family rep is created / `mol ssrecalc` is called** (the STRIDE license notice prints — "please reference: Frishman,D & Argos,P. (1995)") [VERIFIED probe1; CITED UG §6.1.14 "it will be calculated automatically by the program STRIDE"; `vmd-ref/scripts/vmdinit.tcl:136` `vmd_calculate_structure` → `mol ssrecalc`].
- `mol ssrecalc <molid>` **exists** in 1.9.3 [VERIFIED probe1 `SSRECALC_OK`; CITED UG mol command table: "recalculate secondary structure … using the current set of coordinates"].

### 3.2 The ss field — vocabulary and access channels

- **`ss` is NOT an atomselect attribute** — `get ss` and `set ss` both fail ("cannot find attribute 'ss'") [VERIFIED probe1]. The PyMOL-style `ss='L'` assignment of v1 has **no direct VMD analog**.
- The real channels [VERIFIED probe1 keyword dump + probes]:
  - `structure` — per-atom ss letter, values **`T` (turn), `C` (coil), `H` (helix), `G` (3-10), `E` (extended/β), `B` (bridge)**; readable AND **writable** [VERIFIED probe7: `set structure H` succeeded, read-back `H`];
  - boolean selectors `helix`, `sheet`, `coil`, `turn` (read-only — `set` fails) [VERIFIED probe7];
  - `phi`, `psi` per-atom backbone dihedrals (present in the keyword list) [VERIFIED keyword dump].
- **There is no 'L' value in VMD.** The v1 caveat's name is PyMOL vocabulary; the v2 equivalent is "the fake residue gets `T`/`C` and renders as a **tube**".

### 3.3 What ss does a spliced GAM residue get, and what does it render as?

- **`T` (turn)** on 1xdn (resid 101/9001 variants) and 1znf-model-1 (resid 9001) [VERIFIED probe2/probe4/probe6 `hiderCA_structure={... T}`]. A copy-displaced residue has distorted phi/psi → STRIDE says turn/coil → **Cartoon/NewCartoon draw it as a smooth tube** [CITED UG: "all other structures (coils and turns) as a tube"].
- `mol ssrecalc` after the splice leaves it `T` [VERIFIED probe2 `hiderCA_structure_after=T`].
- Neighbor-ripple: when the splice geometry is OVER-displaced (≥1.4 Å), STRIDE reassigns the anchor residue's ss (residue 100 flipped `E→T` in probes 2/3) — a corruption signal, but **not a reliable one** (probe5b PP14 degraded the path with NO ss ripple).

### 3.4 Manual ss override — the probe8 upgrade [VERIFIED — the biggest surprise of this research]

- `$sel set structure H` **works** and **the Cartoon renderer honors it**: writing `H` onto residues 82–87 (were `E`) changed the Cartoon window render from `FCyl 35 STri 68` (sheet ribbons) to `FCyl 41 STri 0` (helix geometry) [VERIFIED probe8].
- Writing `H` on a **single** residue (100) changed nothing (`22/8` before and after) — the UG ≥3-residue helix rule applies to the render regardless of written ss [VERIFIED probe8 + CITED UG §6.1.14].
- **`mol ssrecalc` WIPES manual writes** (82–87 `H→E` after ssrecalc — STRIDE recomputed from geometry) [VERIFIED probe8]. Manual ss and ssrecalc are mutually exclusive choices.
- Persistence caveat: manual ss is script-modified data — lost on any reload (same class as beta/segid, Pitfall 7); load-time STRIDE reassigns `T` anyway. A `.bcm` sidecar would need to carry intended ss values if Phase 20 must restore them.

**Consequence:** the ss='L' caveat is now fully characterized — see the Decision Matrix (§9). The one-line version: *a single-residue fake always renders as a tube (accepted, v1-equivalent look); forcing helix/sheet is possible but only for ≥3-residue fake segments, via `set structure` (NOT via ssrecalc).*

## 4. Q3 — Splice design for the combined PDB

### 4.1 The bond law and the displacement envelope (the governing math)

VMD bonds two atoms iff `distance < 0.6 × (r_i + r_j)` (C=1.70, N=1.55, O=1.52) [CITED — 17.1-RESEARCH-simple-generators.md, boundary-precision verified]. Real peptide C–N ≈ 1.33 Å; C–N cutoff = 0.6×(1.70+1.55) = **1.95 Å**. Displacing the fake residue rigidly by `d` **perpendicular to the peptide bond** stretches the junction to `sqrt(1.33² + d²)`:

| d (Å) | junction distance | bonded? | probe evidence |
|-------|-------------------|---------|----------------|
| 0.0 | 1.33 | ✅ | probe2 A / probe5b SX05 (bits render; note: at 0.5–0.75 the path may *merge* the near-coincident CAs — see 4.4) |
| 0.5–1.0 | 1.47–1.66 | ✅ safe margin | probe2 B, probe5b SX10/PP10 (in-path, all 4 reps) |
| 1.25 | 1.82 | ✅ (marginal) | probe3 SWEEP d=1.25 still rendered |
| 1.44 | 1.96 | ❌ (> 1.95) | probe5b SX14 (bits gone, ss ripple) / PP14 (degraded path) |
| 2.0 | 2.40 | ❌ | probe2 D / probe3 (nothing rendered) |

**Displacement along** the peptide bond adds directly: 1.33 + d ≤ 1.95 → **d ≤ 0.62 Å only**. Direction matters enormously [VERIFIED: probe4 DIRPP (perp to the CA–CA *axis*, 1.0 Å) failed while probe5b PP10 (perp to the *peptide bond*, 1.0 Å) succeeded — the safe direction is **perpendicular to the peptide-bond vector**, not perpendicular to the chain axis].

### 4.2 The recommended GAM residue record spec (probe-exact)

```
Record layout (per fake residue, 78 cols, mirrors mutation.tcl::_hider_record):
  "ATOM  %5d %4s%1s%-3s %1s%4d%1s   %8.3f%8.3f%8.3f%6.2f%6.1f      %-4s%2s"
  serial        sequential, continuing after the real atoms
  name          " N  ", " CA ", " C  ", " O  ", " CB "   (cols 13-16)
  altloc        " "
  resname       GAM                                        (cols 18-20)
  chain         the ANCHOR residue's chain id (e.g. "A")   (col 22)  -- NOT "G"
  resid         9001 + k (disjoint block, one residue per k) (cols 23-26)
  icode         " "
  x y z         anchor-atom coords + (dx,dy,dz)            (cols 31-54)
  occupancy     1.00
  beta          -999.0 on CA ONLY; 0.00 on N/C/O/CB        (cols 61-66; %6.1f → "-999.0" fits)
  segid         GAME (all 5 atoms)                         (cols 73-76)
  element       N / C / C / O / C — copied per-atom        (cols 77-78, ALWAYS emitted)
```

Field-level rules [VERIFIED unless noted]:
- **beta=-999 on CA only.** Probed (probe2 variant H): `resname GAM and beta < 0` returns exactly 1 index; rendering identical to beta-all. This keeps one registry record per hider AND makes the game's VDW hider rep draw ONE sphere at the fake CA (5-sphere blob otherwise) AND keeps `tag_sentinels`-style full-residue beta resets from being needed. **Integration warning:** the existing `tag_sentinels` sets beta on ALL `resname GAM` atoms — the cartoon tier needs a CA-only variant (`resname GAM and name CA` for beta; all GAM atoms for segid), or fetch must add `and name CA`.
- **Element field always emitted** per-atom (N/C/C/O/C) — blank/mangled elements degrade to X (radius 1.50, 12% undersized) [CITED 17.1-RESEARCH-simple-generators.md probe J/K].
- **Coordinate overflow guard:** `%8.3f` breaks at |coord| ≥ 10000 (silently corrupts trailing fields) [CITED 17.1-RESEARCH-simple-generators.md] — guard in the pure layer.
- **Resname GAM (3 chars)** is correct and requires NO sentinel migration — the canonical selector `resname GAM and beta < 0` is unchanged [VERIFIED].
- **No CONECT records.** They are unnecessary at ≤1.0 Å, and probe3's (initially buggy, then serial-fixed) CONECT variants made rendering WORSE (window lost units, Trace drew 0) — VMD's distance search + homogeneity analysis doesn't benefit; avoid [VERIFIED probes I/J + probe3 CONECT d=1.5/2.0].
- **No renumbering of real residues.** Disjoint resid (999/9001) rendered identically to renumbered (probe2 G vs A; probe6 resid 9001) [VERIFIED]. Renumbering is optional hygiene; skipping it avoids touching the real chain's records entirely.
- **Multi-model PDBs:** `writepdb` collapses the 37-model 1znf to **1 frame** on rewrite [VERIFIED probe6: `znf_numframes=37` → `znf_rewrite_numframes=1`] — the Phase 15/16 mutate flow already collapses demos to a single-state molecule, so the splice writer operates on single-model text. No per-model handling needed.
- **Append-at-end == mid-file splice** [VERIFIED probe6 MID vs END: identical GAME-only bits (6 FCyl) and identical window renders] — the cartoon tier can reuse `write_combined_pdb`'s "writepdb + append records + END" flow with a multi-atom record builder appended at the end.

### 4.4 Subtleties measured

- **0.5–0.75 Å displacements may path-merge**: at SX05 the window counts matched the 4-unit (GAM-excluded) level while bits still rendered — near-coincident CAs get merged by the spline builders. **1.0 Å is the sweet spot** (unambiguously in-path) [VERIFIED probe5b SX05 vs SX10].
- **Half-bonded is worse than unbonded:** at PP14 (1.44 Å ⊥) one junction survived → a dangling branch that DISRUPTED the path builder (window dropped to 3-unit counts, below even the 4-unit baseline) [VERIFIED]. The generator must keep BOTH junctions bonded (d ≤ ~1.2 Å) — never "mostly bonded".
- **ss ripple is not a reliable breakage signal** (PP14 degraded with clean ss) [VERIFIED]. The headless render-diff (§6) is the real check.

## 5. Q4 — Trace/Tube sidestep viability

- **They are SS-independent** [CITED UG §6.1.10/6.1.11] — no STRIDE shape; Trace = CA cylinders, Tube = smooth CA spline.
- **They have the SAME connectivity requirement** — an unbonded/over-displaced GAM renders NOTHING under Trace/Tube just as under Cartoon (probe2 C/D: Tube window counts == baseline; bonded variants: Tube +segments) [VERIFIED]. The sidestep idea only ever addressed the *ss* question, not connectivity.
- **The same splice serves all four reps** — one GAM residue produced bits under Cartoon (6 FCyl), NewCartoon (14 TriStrip) and Tube (6 FCyl) simultaneously, and the window renders show path inclusion for Cartoon, NewCartoon, Trace AND Tube at 1.0 Å [VERIFIED probe5b/probe6]. **The "sidestep" is therefore not an either/or: it is a rep choice over shared machinery.**
- **Visual fidelity cost:** for a single-residue hider there is essentially NO fidelity difference — Cartoon/NewCartoon draw it as a coil/turn TUBE anyway (ss=T), which is what Tube/Trace draw (probe6/probe5b: per-residue bit counts comparable). The reps differ only on real helices/sheets — the hider bump looks alike everywhere.
- Conclusion: Trace/Tube need NOT replace Cartoon/NewCartoon; generate for all four from one splice (they are all in GAME_REPS, `setup_state.tcl:10`).

## 7. Q6 — Scoring/pick mapping for a multi-atom hider residue

### 7.1 Which atom index is "the hider"?

- The pick contract delivers the clicked atom's 0-based index (`vmd_pick_atom`, LOCKED per AGENTS.md — 16-12/16-16/16-17). A click on a cartoon/tube geometry resolves to a **backbone atom** of the residue under the cursor [CITED UG §6.1.14 for helix cylinders: "possible to pick the Cα for each cylinder segment"; **coil-tube pick target UNVERIFIED** — could be N, CA, or C].
- **Design: register the CA index only** (one record per hider — beta=-999 on CA only, fetch selector UNCHANGED) **+ a per-residue fallback lookup at pick time**:
  1. `on_pick(idx)`: if `registry::is_hider idx` → score (unchanged Phase 16 path);

## 8. PITFALLS discovered (new, this phase)

### Pitfall C1: Over-displacement silently degrades — half-bonded is WORSE than unbonded
**What:** displacing the fake residue ≥1.44 Å ⊥ peptide leaves ONE junction bonded → a dangling branch that corrupts the path builder (window render dropped to 3-unit counts, below the 4-unit baseline; probe5b PP14). Along-bond displacement breaks at ≥0.62 Å.
**Why:** bond law `d < 0.6(r_i+r_j)` — the junction (1.33 Å) hits the 1.95 Å C–N cutoff at d=1.43 perpendicular, 0.62 along.
**Avoid:** rigid-translate the whole residue ≤1.0 Å perpendicular to the peptide-bond vector; verify BOTH junctions in the smoke (render-diff, not ss).
**Warning signs:** GAME-only bits render but window counts DROP below the control; ss ripple on the anchor residue (sometimes).

### Pitfall C2: `mol ssrecalc` wipes manual ss — and is unnecessary for the splice
**What:** ssrecalc re-runs STRIDE, overwriting any `set structure` writes (probe8: H→E) and re-deriving T for GAM anyway.
**Avoid:** do NOT call ssrecalc in the cartoon generator flow; if manual ss is used (future multi-residue polish), never ssrecalc afterwards, and persist intended ss in the .bcm sidecar (script data is lost on reload, Pitfall 7 class).

### Pitfall C3: `ss` does not exist as an atomselect attribute
**What:** `get ss` / `set ss` fail; v1-style ss manipulation has no direct port. Use `structure` (read/write), `helix`/`sheet`/`coil`/`turn` (read-only booleans), `phi`/`psi`.
**Avoid:** never write `atomselect ... set ss`; test ss via `structure`.

### Pitfall C4: Displacement direction is as important as magnitude
**What:** 1.0 Å perpendicular to the CA–CA axis FAILED while 1.0 Å perpendicular to the peptide BOND succeeded (probe4 DIRPP vs probe5b PP10) — a displacement ⊥ to the chain axis can be nearly ALONG the peptide bond, breaking it.
**Avoid:** compute the displacement vector perpendicular to the local peptide-bond vector `unit(C_anchor − N_next)`; handle the degenerate parallel case (pick any non-parallel second vector).

### Pitfall C5: 0.5–0.75 Å bumps may path-merge (invisible hider)
**What:** near-coincident fake CAs get merged by the spline builders (probe5b SX05/SX075 rendered bits but the window behaved as GAM-excluded).
**Avoid:** default 1.0 Å; treat <0.75 Å as "not verified in-path".

### Pitfall C6: The cartoon tier must NOT reuse `tag_sentinels` or the sphere-tier record builder as-is
**What:** `tag_sentinels` sets beta=-999 on ALL `resname GAM` atoms (destroys CA-only design → 5 fetch indices → 5× counts); `_hider_record` writes single atoms with chain "G"/resid 9001 (wrong chain breaks the trace — separate fragment; fixed resid collides across multiple hiders).
**Avoid:** a cartoon-aware tag (beta on `resname GAM and name CA`, segid on all) and a multi-atom residue record builder with the anchor's chain + a per-hider resid from the 9001+k block.

### Pitfall C7: CONECT records — unnecessary and risky
**What:** CONECT neither rescued over-displacement (fixed-serial probe3 still degraded) nor improved ≤1.0 Å splices; a buggy CONECT (serial off-by-insertion-shift) actively corrupted rendering.
**Avoid:** rely on the distance search; never emit CONECT for hider records.

### Pitfall C8: Chain id mismatch = v1's disconnected failure mode (ASSUMED — verify once)
**What:** a fake residue on a DIFFERENT chain (e.g. the sphere-tier's `G`) forms its own fragment → not traced. All probed successes used the anchor's chain; the mismatch case is unprobed but follows from fragment homogeneity [CITED UG §4.2].
**Avoid:** copy the anchor residue's chain id; add a negative smoke assertion (chain 'G' splice → 0 render bits).
